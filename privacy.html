Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

PDFfun ("PDFfun", "we", "us", or "our") provides online tools for working with PDF and document files. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the PDFfun website and its services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PDFfun, we may receive limited information such as information you provide voluntarily, technical information about your browser or device, and information necessary to operate and improve the website.

We do not require you to create an account simply to use the basic PDF tools available on the website.

2. Your PDF and Document Files

Some PDFfun tools are designed to process files directly within your web browser. When a tool performs processing locally in your browser, the file does not need to be uploaded to a PDFfun server for that processing.

However, not every current or future PDFfun feature necessarily works in the same way. Some advanced services may require a file to be transmitted to a processing server. Where this is necessary, the applicable tool or service should provide appropriate information about the processing.

You should avoid uploading confidential, sensitive or highly personal documents unless you understand how the particular tool processes the file.

3. File Storage

Browser-based processing may use temporary browser memory or storage while a tool is operating. Such data is generally controlled by your browser and may be removed when the page or browser session ends.

If future PDFfun services introduce server-side file processing or storage, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain those practices.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PDFfun may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, analytics or other legitimate website purposes.

You can generally control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics services in the future to understand website traffic, usage patterns and performance. These services may collect information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited and interaction data.

Any analytics or advertising services added to PDFfun may have their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Third-Party Services

PDFfun may use third-party libraries, hosting providers, analytics providers, content delivery networks or other services to operate the website.

Third-party services may process technical information or requests according to their own policies.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website and its services. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

PDFfun is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or profiles.

9. External Links

PDFfun may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing information to them.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete or restrict certain processing.

Requests relating to personal information can be directed to PDFfun using the contact information made available on the website.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as PDFfun develops, introduces new features or changes how information is handled. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PDFfun's privacy practices, please contact us through the contact information provided on the website.
